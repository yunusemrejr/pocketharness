# Natural Editorial Writing

Use for reports, product copy, explanations, outreach drafts and editing. Preserve meaning and precision; this is not an AI-detector evasion workflow.

## Working method

- Identify audience, purpose, channel and desired action.
- Build around concrete facts and a coherent sequence of ideas.
- Match voice to the user’s examples and context.
- Edit for clarity, accuracy and rhythm rather than mechanical word bans.

Read [patterns and examples](references/patterns.md) only for the relevant writing problem. Inspect the user's examples, existing document and authoritative sources before choosing voice, structure or claims. User instructions take precedence; writing a draft does not authorize sending or publishing it.

## Evidence and completion

Keep the intended reader/action, factual sources and established voice together. Draft the shortest complete argument, verify claims/names/numbers/links, then make one focused editorial pass. Reuse completed source checks and unchanged passages; do not add extra reviewers or repeated rewrites unless a concrete uncertainty or feedback justifies them. Preserve meaningful terminology, quotations and mandated wording instead of applying mechanical word bans.

When an artifact exists, `pocket kit quality DOC.md` provides bounded mechanical leads from the installed lint/slop checks. Inspect each cue in context; a scan is not fact-checking, a tone judgment or evidence that writing was AI-authored. Verify the final document's actual facts and reader task. Deliver the requested draft/file, with unsupported claims removed or explicitly qualified and any material source gap stated. A simple paragraph can finish directly; consequential public claims may require targeted source/reviewer attention.

## When stock phrasing is flagged
Read the surrounding paragraph, audience brief and factual source. Several generic phrases are a prompt to check substance, not a forbidden-word list. Rewrite only where meaning becomes clearer. Preserve quotations, mandated wording and necessary technical repetition. Never fill an evidence gap with an invented example presented as fact. One focused revision is usually enough; do not spend repeated turns polishing equivalent sentences.
