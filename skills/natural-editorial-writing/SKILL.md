# Natural Editorial Writing

Use for reports, product copy, explanations, outreach drafts and editing. Preserve meaning and precision; this is not an AI-detector evasion workflow.

## Working method

- Identify audience, purpose, channel and desired action.
- Build around concrete facts and a coherent sequence of ideas.
- Match voice to the user’s examples and context.
- Edit for clarity, accuracy and rhythm rather than mechanical word bans.

Read [patterns and examples](references/patterns.md) for the relevant implementation mode; do not load unrelated modes. Inspect the actual runtime, project conventions and constraints before choosing syntax, dependencies or deployment steps. User instructions take precedence; this skill adds no authority to change external systems.

## Evidence and completion

Use a small representative case and the relevant failure case to check the result. Report what was executed, what remains unverified, and any material compatibility assumption. Do not invent measured outcomes or treat reading this guide as verification.

## When stock phrasing is flagged
Read the surrounding paragraph, audience brief and factual source. Several generic phrases are a prompt to check substance, not a forbidden-word list. Rewrite only where meaning becomes clearer. Preserve quotations, mandated wording and necessary technical repetition. Never fill an evidence gap with an invented example presented as fact. One focused revision is usually enough; do not spend repeated turns polishing equivalent sentences.
