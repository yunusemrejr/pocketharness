# Financial statement analysis

Use for reading annual or quarterly filings, building comparable financial tables, explaining cash conversion, or investigating accounting inconsistencies. Start with the entity, reporting framework, fiscal periods, presentation currency and requested analysis. A ticker alone is insufficient when listings, subsidiaries or share classes differ.

Use the filed statements and notes as the accounting source of truth; retain accession or filing identifiers, publication dates, period boundaries, units and page or XBRL references. Separate reported amounts, deterministic calculations and analyst assumptions. Do not replace unavailable values with zero or silently combine amended and original filings.

Read [filing reconciliation](references/filing-reconciliation.md) when extracting or normalizing statements, and [operating analysis](references/operating-analysis.md) for ratios, cash-flow bridges and comparability. Load only the relevant guide.

Check balance-sheet and cash-flow identities before interpreting trends. For each adjustment, retain its reported starting value, reason, amount, sign and resulting value. Use scenarios for uncertain classifications and explain which conclusions survive them. Compare like periods and reconcile currency and scale before arithmetic.

Deliver source-linked tables with formulas, important accounting-policy differences, unexplained residuals and limitations. This skill supports analysis; it does not authorize trades, purchases or account access. Refresh sources for current financial claims rather than presenting a historical filing as current performance.
