# Extracting and reconciling filings

Identify the legal reporting entity and consolidation perimeter first. Capture reporting basis, audit or review status, fiscal year-end, reporting currency, scale, restatement status and retrieval date. Keep a raw fact record such as `{entity, concept, value, unit, start, end, filed, accession, dimensions, source}` beside the normalized table. A comparative figure in a later amended filing can legitimately differ from the original; choose a version policy and record the change rather than averaging values.

For US filings, EDGAR submissions and extracted XBRL are useful discovery and structured-data channels. Company Facts is not a replacement for the original footnotes. Inspect the filing when extension tags, dimensions or accounting presentation matter. Follow the SEC's current access requirements rather than hardcoding guessed request limits. [SEC API documentation](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) and [developer resources](https://www.sec.gov/about/developer-resources).

Distinguish instant observations, such as year-end cash, from duration observations, such as six-month revenue. Match start and end dates, not merely a fiscal-year field. Nine-month cash flow is usually cumulative; derive a stand-alone third quarter only by subtracting compatible six-month figures. Never subtract a balance-sheet instant from a duration flow. A trailing-year flow can be annual plus current year-to-date minus prior comparable year-to-date when all three share the same accounting perimeter and policy. Describe acquisition or restatement breaks explicitly.

Normalize signs deliberately. An expense may be stored as a positive presentation amount while a cash outflow is negative. Keep both raw and analytical signs to avoid a double negation. Verify scale before converting thousands to millions, and retain currency codes rather than ambiguous dollar symbols. Ratios combining stock and flow quantities often need average balances. If only opening and closing balances are available, label the approximation and consider seasonality.

Reconcile assets minus liabilities minus equity with an explicit treatment of noncontrolling interests. Reconcile opening cash plus operating, investing and financing changes plus foreign-exchange and other permitted reconciliation items to closing cash. Define whether cash includes restricted cash for the particular statement. A nonzero residual requires investigation; rounding tolerance should follow the source's displayed precision, not an arbitrary percentage chosen to hide discrepancies.

Use a provenance table to resolve duplicate facts: prefer the intended filing and period, then matching units and dimensions, then the selected restatement policy. Do not simply keep the largest value or the latest timestamp across incompatible periods. Keep segment totals separate from consolidated totals and document eliminations. For automated extraction, test a restatement, a non-calendar fiscal year, a cumulative interim flow, an extension tag and a currency-scale mismatch. Return unresolved mappings for review rather than manufacturing completeness.
