# Investment risk analysis

Use for a requested portfolio risk report, instrument valuation comparison, sensitivity study or historical backtest. Establish the valuation date, instrument identifiers, position quantities, currencies, return convention, benchmark and horizon. Separate a hypothetical research portfolio from an actual account; analysis is not authorization to trade or retrieve private account data.

Read [valuation and units](references/valuation-units.md) for cash flows, discounting, equity and bond scenarios. Read [portfolio risk](references/portfolio-risk.md) for covariance, tail loss, drawdowns and backtest controls.

Keep observed market inputs separate from assumptions and derived outputs. Date prices, exchange rates, corporate actions and yield curves; record missing and stale observations. Value positions in a consistent base currency before aggregating. Explain whether returns include distributions, fees, financing and tax assumptions.

Report a range of outcomes under transparent scenarios rather than suggesting a model guarantees a result. Test arithmetic identities, sign conventions and limiting cases; show sensitivity to parameters that drive the answer. Do not confuse historical volatility with maximum possible loss or interpret a backtest as proof of future profitability.

Deliver calculations with source references, units, valuation dates and model limitations. For personalized recommendations, establish the relevant constraints and distinguish educational analysis from suitability judgments. Never turn this skill into autonomous order placement or portfolio rebalancing.
