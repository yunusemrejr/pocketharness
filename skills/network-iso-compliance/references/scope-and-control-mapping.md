# Standards scope and a defensible control map

## Select the assessment object

An ISMS assessment concerns how an organization manages information-security risk within a defined boundary. A network architecture review concerns controls and exposure in a particular technical environment. They overlap, but neither can substitute for the other. Record legal entities, locations, systems, outsourced services, interfaces and exclusions before assigning coverage. A corporate certificate may not cover every office, product or cloud account. Use the actual declared scope rather than the organization name alone.

The official [ISO/IEC 27001:2022 catalog](https://www.iso.org/standard/27001) describes ISMS requirements. [ISO/IEC 27002:2022](https://www.iso.org/standard/75652.html) provides information-security control guidance. Verify amendments and the edition selected by the organization at assessment time. Do not assume that a clause number from a 2013 document remains the same in a 2022 mapping. An official catalog description establishes identity and purpose, not the full licensed requirement text.

For a network design task, check the applicable ISO/IEC 27033 part and its current catalog status. The family covers different subjects, including scenarios and wireless access. For example, [ISO/IEC 27033-6:2016](https://www.iso.org/standard/51585.html) addresses wireless IP network access. A part's older publication year does not automatically mean withdrawn, and one confirmed part does not prove every part is current. Record the exact identifier actually used. Do not silently promote an informative technical guide into a certification requirement.

## Build a traceable matrix

Use columns for standard/edition, requirement reference, organization control, applicability rationale, risk, evidence requested, evidence received, test performed, result, limitation and owner. Populate exact clause references only from the organization's legitimate standard copy or an authoritative supplied mapping. A practical evidence item might be a firewall-change record, approved segmentation diagram or periodic access-review result. Keep its repository path, date and accountable owner so reviewers can reopen the source.

Treat the Statement of Applicability and risk-treatment records as organization-specific decisions to examine, not boilerplate to generate from scan findings. A port exposed by design might be accepted with compensating controls; an apparently closed port does not prove the surrounding process is effective. Identify what the control is intended to achieve and what evidence would demonstrate its operation over time. Distinguish design adequacy, implementation and operating effectiveness.

## Avoid artificial certainty

Do not mark a control satisfied merely because a product is installed, nor failed because one evidence file is unavailable. Record whether an item is implemented, partially evidenced, not applicable with rationale, or unresolved pending information. Audit terminology should follow the engagement's agreed methodology. Where a standard or contract is unavailable, produce a proposed mapping for review and label that dependency. The result should make the scope and reasoning inspectable without presenting the agent's interpretation as the normative text.
