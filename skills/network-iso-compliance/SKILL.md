---
name: network-iso-compliance
description: Map network-security evidence to the organization’s stated ISO/IEC scope and controls, prepare gap assessments and audit evidence, and distinguish technical findings from certification.
---

Establish the organization, assessment boundary, standard edition and intended deliverable: internal gap assessment, control evidence, readiness review or support for an independent audit. Honor the user's existing authorization. Do not equate a network scan with an information-security management-system assessment or certification.

Use [scope-and-control-mapping](references/scope-and-control-mapping.md) to select the relevant standard family and build a traceable evidence matrix. Use [evidence-and-remediation](references/evidence-and-remediation.md) for sampling, findings, remediation ownership and retesting.

Verify editions, amendments and lifecycle status against official catalogs when the task depends on them. ISO/IEC 27001:2022 concerns ISMS requirements; ISO/IEC 27002:2022 supplies control guidance. Network-specific standards may support an assessment but do not replace the organization's risk treatment and declared applicability. Read licensed clauses only from material the user can legitimately provide; do not invent clause numbers or reproduce copyrighted standards text.

Separate requirement, organization interpretation, observed evidence, gap and recommendation. Treat missing evidence as an assessment limitation rather than automatic proof of nonconformity. Technical observations should have dates, sources, scope and confidence; management-system conclusions also need process and governance evidence.

Deliver a reviewable matrix and prioritized actions with owners and acceptance criteria. Label an internal readiness report accurately and never claim that a tool or agent has issued ISO certification.
