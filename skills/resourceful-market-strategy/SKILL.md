# Resourceful Market Strategy

Use for positioning, customer understanding, messaging and low-resource acquisition experiments. Persuasion should clarify value, not invent urgency or exploit vulnerability.

## Working method

- Identify a concrete audience, need, alternative and buying context.
- Separate observed evidence from assumptions about motives.
- Choose one affordable experiment with an outcome metric.
- Write truthful claims and learn from qualified responses, not vanity counts.

Read [patterns and examples](references/patterns.md) for the relevant implementation mode; do not load unrelated modes. ## Search-first content surfaces

Start every text surface from what the audience searches for; one clear purpose per page (**one answer, many surfaces**), judged by valuable action, never impressions. See [search-first content surfaces](references/patterns.md#search-first-content-surfaces) for the working method; use [organic growth engineering](../organic-growth-engineering/SKILL.md) for site structure and [search discoverability](../search-discoverability/SKILL.md) to verify visibility.

## Campaigns, ads and channel economics

- **Unit economics first**: know contribution margin per customer and target payback before spending; a channel is viable when CAC stays under the margin-based ceiling at the volume you need (`financial-modeling`).
- **Ad and landing copy**: one audience, one pain or desire, one promise, one proof, one action. The headline states the outcome in the customer's words; the proof is specific (a number with its basis, a named customer with permission, a demo); the CTA says what happens next. Ad and landing page must match message and visual so the click lands where it was promised (`copywriting`).
- **Creative testing**: vary one element at a time (hook, offer, visual), keep budgets equal, and judge on cost per qualified action, not clicks. Kill losers fast; iterate on the winner's hook.
- **Attribution caveats**: platform-reported conversions over-count; compare with first-party data (orders, signups by source) and use holdout or geo tests for large budgets.
- **Honesty**: no fake scarcity, invented reviews, fabricated numbers or dark patterns; they win a test and lose the brand.

## Evidence and completion

Use a small representative case and the relevant failure case to check the result. Report what was executed, what remains unverified, and any material compatibility assumption. Do not invent measured outcomes or treat reading this guide as verification.
