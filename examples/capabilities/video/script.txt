Most people back up their laptop once, and never test the restore. Here's the problem: a backup you have never restored is only a hope, not a plan.

The rule is three, two, one. Three copies of your data. On two different kinds of storage. With one copy kept somewhere else.

Then, once a month, actually restore a file. If it opens, your backup works. If it doesn't, you just found out while it still matters.
